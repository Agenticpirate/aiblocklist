## 1. Epistemological Grounding & Hard Product Boundaries

AIBlockList.com (*Identify. Verify. Filter.*) is independent, open-source trust-and-safety infrastructure for identifying, verifying, aggregating, and filtering **observable abusive automated social media behavior**.

### Non-Negotiable Architectural Safeguards (Locked)
1. **No automatic public classification** based on community reports.
2. **No AI-only moderation** and **no AI-writing detection** (writing style, grammar, tone, English proficiency, third-party AI detector scores) as standalone evidence — grounded in Liang et al. (2023, *Patterns*, $p = 9.74 \times 10^{-5}$ non-native English bias; trivial evasion to 13% via self-edit prompts).
3. **Mandatory human review**, plus **mandatory dual independent review** for high-impact accounts and **independent second-reviewer adjudication** for all appeals.
4. **Immutable cryptographic audit history** (`audit_logs` SHA-256 hash chain) and transparent, non-silent source attribution (`source_assertions[]` with `has_source_conflict`).
5. **Privacy-first evidence storage** — canonical public URLs, SHA-256 hashes, numerical behavioral metrics, and local embeddings; zero raw verbatim third-party post archiving and zero user-uploaded screenshots in MVP.
6. **Official API integrations only** where platform policies permit; targeted, evidence-first lookups to respect X's 2026 pay-per-use pricing (\$0.005/post read, \$0.010/user read, 2M–3M monthly self-serve cap) and mandatory deletion rules.
7. **No automated mass blocking or DOM-clicking** against native social platform buttons (eliminating X anti-scripting suspension risk).
8. **Open-source-first architecture** with strict separation between Code Governance and Trust & Safety Governance, and three Immutable Charter Red Lines (never sell private user/reporter data, never charge for appeals/delisting, never paywall core DB/transparency/code).

### Behavioral Spectrum Boundary
| Zone | Categories | Policy Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **Protected Non-Spam** | (1) Purely human-written; (2) AI-assisted human communication (translation, grammar, drafting); (3) AI-generated content with meaningful human review & contextual engagement | Explicitly protected; eligible for `cleared` status + anti-harassment cooldown |
| **Actionable Abusive Automation** | (4) Fully automated spam; (5) Repetitive automated engagement farming; (6) Coordinated spam campaigns; (7) Malicious bot networks; (8) Impersonation & deceptive automation | Evaluated strictly via versioned behavioral signals (`SIG-01`–`SIG-05`) and human review |

## 3. Unresolved Ambiguities & External Dependencies Extracted from the 72-Page PDF

Across all 20 Document Gates in the Master Research Report, the following items are explicitly **uncertain or externally gated** and must NOT be guessed or silently hardcoded during implementation:

1. **X API Economics & Access:** Whether X approves AIBlockList for free **Public Utility App** status; whether X's self-serve monthly read cap is strictly **3M** (4 Oct 2026 guides) or **2M** (1 guide); real-world billing verification in X Developer Console.
2. **Backend Language Trade-off:** **FastAPI (Python 3.12+)** vs. **NestJS (TypeScript)** — spec recommends FastAPI for native local embeddings/NLP, while noting the single-language TS contributor trade-off.
3. **Legal & Regulatory Determinations (Requires Qualified Counsel):**
   - Formal enforceability of **GDPR Article 6(1)(f) Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA)** for public behavioral blocklists.
   - **India DPDP Rules 2025** inactivity retention scope (Gazette 3-year schedule for notified platform classes vs. 1-year general retention summary) and 8th Schedule language compliance ahead of May 13, 2027.
   - **US 47 U.S.C. § 230(c)(2)** immunity boundaries and defamation defense posture (*e360 Insight v. Spamhaus*).
   - Whether **SHA-256 content hashes and 384-dim embeddings** are fully exempt from X's mandatory deletion rules and GDPR Article 17 erasure or must also be purged/tombstoned upon deletion requests.
   - **Legal Entity & Jurisdiction:** Non-Profit Foundation / Fiscal Sponsor vs. Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) / LLC, plus hosting region (EU, US, or India).
4. **Calibration & Thresholds:**
   - Empirical precision/recall of `SIG-01` (0.90 cosine similarity) and `SIG-02` (5-thread template reuse) on live X replies — gated on the **Phase 0 200-account benchmark** (<2% FP on non-native English writers).
   - Exact `is_high_impact` follower threshold (**10,000 vs. 25,000**).
   - Appeal verification economics: **X OAuth vs. public bio-token challenge** long-term cost and UX.
5. **External Source Licensing & Initial Seed:**
   - Open-source licenses (if any) of `blockedforaireply.levels.io`, `Clankwall`, `x-slop.com`, and `STOP SLOP` are currently unverified; ingestion cannot be enabled until verified and approved.
   - Phase 1 seed choice: exclusively 100–250 freshly hand-verified core records vs. also importing external lists tagged `unverified_external`.
6. **Phase 3–5 Scale & Platform Unknowns:**
   - `pgvector` HNSW index memory scaling past 2M embeddings and `pg_trgm` vs. Meilisearch CE benchmark at 50K–500K accounts.
   - R2 Class B operation costs at 250K+ extensions without CDN cache shielding.
   - Exact 2026 per-endpoint rate limits / privileged intents / commercial API terms for Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Reddit, Threads, and Discord (must be re-verified directly against primary platform docs before Phase 5).
   - Creator willingness to connect voluntary analytics OAuth (gating the optional Creator Protection Dashboard).
   - Budget/vendor selection for pre-Phase-3 external penetration testing and initial staffing for vetted volunteer moderators, Appeals Panel, and Conduct Committee.
